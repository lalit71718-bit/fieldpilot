How it works

One conversation. The whole day handled.

Every request follows the same loop: know the context, understand the need, act through real tools, then follow up.

  1. 01

    Before

    “What’s on today?”

    Reads the family calendar, open chores, reminders and household preferences, and briefs you the moment the call starts.

  2. 02

    During

    “The AC shows E4 again.”

    Checks the service history, spots that this is the third E4 in 64 days, and walks you through one safe check from the manual.

  3. 03

    Action

    “Get someone to fix it.”

    Finds technician slots, skips the one that clashes with Dadi’s doctor visit, and books only after your spoken “yes”.

  4. 04

    After

    “Update the family.”

    Adds the visit to the calendar with a reminder, assigns the grocery run, and posts a summary to the family channel.

Built for trust

An assistant that acts in your home must never act wrongly.

  • Human in the loop. Anything that costs money waits for a spoken “yes”.
  • “No” means no, in code. If your last words were a refusal, the action is cancelled even if the AI tries to approve it.
  • No double actions. Saying it twice never books twice or adds the same item again.
  • Honest about itself. It never invents features, times or confirmations it didn’t really do.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

Is this real or a mock-up?

The voice, the AI and every action are real and running. The family, calendar and appliance records are demo data stored in a database, and Slack messages are really sent. Each service can be swapped for a live API (Google Calendar, WhatsApp, a booking platform) without changing the assistant.

What happens if it mishears me?

Anything that costs money is read back and waits for your “yes”. Saying “no”, “wait” or “don’t” cancels it, and that rule is enforced in code, not left to the AI.

Which AI does it use?

Open models on Groq (gpt-oss-120b, with a smaller one as backup) and Gemini as a second provider. If one is slow or busy, HomePilot switches instantly so you never hear silence.

Can my family use it too?

Yes. Any family member can be invited into the live call from their phone, gets their own tasks and reminders, and sees updates in the family channel.

What can’t it do?

It doesn’t book travel, place phone calls or shop online yet. It tells you so plainly instead of pretending.
