02

Every request runs the same loop.

Before

It already knows the house.

Each turn starts with live context: who lives here, today’s calendar, open chores, scheduled reminders and household rules like “no visits after 8 PM”.

During

It listens like a person.

Agora’s Conversational AI handles speech, turn-taking and interruptions, and waits when you say “hold on”. Answers stay short because they’re spoken.

Action

It uses real tools, not guesses.

Calendar, chores, shopping list, technician booking and reminders are tools with rules. Anything that costs money waits for your “yes”.

After

It closes the loop.

Reminders ring at the right time, finished chores are ticked off, and the family gets one summary instead of ten messages.

04

Guardrails live in code, not in the prompt.

Language models can misread a “no”. So the decision isn’t left to the model: before any booking runs, the server checks what you actually said.

# backend/tools.py
def _said_no(text: str) -> bool:
    words = set(text.lower().split())
    neg = bool(words & NO_WORDS)    # no, don't, wait, cancel…
    pos = bool(words & YES_WORDS)   # yes, okay, go ahead…
    return neg and not pos

if approved and _said_no(last_user_text):
    approved = False              # the model doesn't get the last word
