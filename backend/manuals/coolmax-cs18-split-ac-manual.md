# CoolMax CS-18 Inverter Split AC — Owner's Manual (troubleshooting excerpt)

## Safety
Before cleaning or checking anything, switch the AC off with the remote and then switch off the AC breaker (MCB) at the distribution board. Never open the outdoor unit's electrical cover yourself: the fan capacitor stores charge even after power is off. Electrical repairs must be done by an authorised technician.

## Error codes shown on the indoor display
- E1: Indoor temperature sensor fault. Call service.
- E2: Indoor fan motor fault. Check that nothing blocks the indoor fan, then call service.
- E3: Low refrigerant or gas leak suspected. Call service; do not keep running.
- E4: Outdoor unit overheating / high pressure protection. The outdoor unit cannot release heat. Most common causes: (1) the outdoor fan does not spin because its run capacitor is weak, (2) the outdoor coil is blocked by dust, leaves or stored items, (3) the outdoor unit is in direct afternoon sun with no ventilation. E4 that keeps coming back in hot afternoons usually means a weak outdoor fan capacitor.
- E5: Voltage too low or too high. Use a stabiliser.
- E6: Communication error between indoor and outdoor unit. Call service.

## What you can safely check for E4
Step 1. Switch off the AC and the breaker. Wait 5 minutes.
Step 2. Look at the outdoor unit from a safe place. Remove anything blocking the air within 50 cm (boxes, plants, covers).
Step 3. Switch the breaker and AC back on in cooling mode. Watch the outdoor fan for 2 minutes. If the compressor hums but the fan does not spin, or spins very slowly, the fan capacitor is likely weak: book a technician. Do not keep resetting the AC, as running with a stuck fan can damage the compressor.
Step 4. If the fan spins normally and the coil is clear, clean the indoor filters and run on 26 degrees. If E4 returns, book a technician.

## Service and warranty
Outdoor fan capacitor replacement typically takes 30 minutes. Keep the model number (CS-18) and installation date ready when booking. Inverter compressor warranty is 5 years from installation.
