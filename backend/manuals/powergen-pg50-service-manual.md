# PowerGen PG-50 Diesel Generator — Field Service Manual (excerpt)

## Safety
Stop the engine and wait 15 minutes before opening the radiator cap. Hot coolant under pressure can cause severe burns. Set the controller to OFF and remove the start key.

## Warning codes
- W1 Low coolant level: top up with 50/50 coolant mix (part CLT-5L, dilute 1:1 with clean water). Check radiator hoses and the drain valve for leaks. If coolant drops again within 24 hours, pressure test the cooling system.
- W2 Low oil pressure: check oil level on dipstick, top up with 15W-40. If pressure stays low, stop and escalate.
- W3 Battery charger failure: check charger fuse and AC supply.
- W4 Overspeed: check governor linkage.

## 250-hour service
Replace engine oil and oil filter, replace fuel filter, check belt tension, check coolant concentration with a refractometer (target -30 C protection), test battery.
