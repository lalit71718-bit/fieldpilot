# AquaWash 7 kg Front Load Washing Machine — Owner's Manual (excerpt)

## Error codes
- dE: Door not closed. Push the door firmly until it clicks; check nothing is caught in the seal.
- OE: Water not draining. Switch off, then clean the drain filter behind the small flap at the bottom front. Keep a towel ready for water.
- IE: Water not filling. Check that the tap is open and the inlet hose is not bent.
- UE: Unbalanced load. Spread the clothes evenly or remove a heavy item and restart the spin.

## Care
Clean the drain filter every 3 months. Run an empty hot cycle with descaler once a month in hard-water areas.
